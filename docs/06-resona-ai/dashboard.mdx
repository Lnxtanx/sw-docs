# Resona Dashboard AI

**Resona Dashboard AI** is your dedicated live dashboard analytics and visualization partner. Anchored beside your database dashboards, it transforms complex relational data into intuitive visual cards, runs autonomous multi-query diagnostic loops, and lets you craft or refine business dashboards entirely through conversation.

![Resona Dashboard AI side panel open next to live dashboard charts with quick prompts and Deep mode query options](/images/de/resona-ai/ds-ai/dashabord-ai.png)

> Quick links: [Dashboard Guide](/data-explorer/dashboard) · [Resona AI Chat](/resona-ai/ai-chat) · [Memory Settings](/resona-ai/memory)

---

## 1 — What Dashboard AI Can Do

Rather than requiring you to hand-craft SQL aggregations or manually drag BI widgets, Dashboard AI handles the entire analytics lifecycle:

- **Generate Full Dashboards**: Analyzes your database and builds complete, thematic dashboards with KPI cards, growth curves, cohort grids, and distribution charts.
- **Modify in Place**: Add new cards, drop obsolete panels, change grouping dimensions, or adjust chart types conversationally.
- **Surface Executive Recommendations**: Generates **Executive Read** cards that pair headline metrics with plain-English diagnoses and concrete tactical next steps.
- **Diagnose Anomalies**: Ask why a metric dropped or spiked, and Dashboard AI will write and run targeted SQL queries to pinpoint the root cause.
- **Keyboard Shortcut**: Press `Ctrl` / `Cmd` + `J` anywhere in Dashboard mode to instantly toggle the Dashboard AI panel.

### Example Prompts

Get started immediately with the quick suggestion chips or type your own instructions:

> *"What are our primary revenue drivers and growth trends?"*  
> *"Summarize overall business health and customer retention"*  
> *"Highlight key executive KPIs and weekly performance"*  
> *"Add a breakdown of active learners by school board and grade level"*  
> *"Why did signups dip in the first week of September?"*

## 2 — Autonomous Multi-Query Execution

When asked to generate or update a dashboard, Resona Dashboard AI doesn't guess column names or generate static mockups. It executes an autonomous, multi-step agentic plan:

![Resona Dashboard AI generating a business analytics dashboard showing the transparent execution trace of schema context retrieval and 13 database queries](/images/de/resona-ai/ds-ai/ds-generated.png)

### The Agentic Loop

1. **`Get Schema Context`**: Resona introspects your active PostgreSQL tables, data types, primary keys, foreign-key relationships, and enum definitions.
2. **Planned Query Batches**: The AI plans an optimal set of analytical queries (e.g. `Ran 13 queries`), grouping calculations by business domain.
3. **`Query Database` Trace**: Each database probe executes through your local [Connector](/connector) in strictly read-only mode. You can expand the trace to inspect individual SQL queries, execution latency, and row results.
4. **Card & Layout Assembly**: The AI formats the results into appropriate visualization types (line charts, area trends, donut breakdowns, or narrative cards) and mounts them into your active dashboard.

## 3 — File & Document Attachments

Dashboard AI is multimodal. Using the **`+` Attach** button beside the prompt bar, you can upload external reference documents directly into your conversation:

![Dashboard AI input bar highlighting the attachment tooltip supporting PDF, CSV, Excel, SQL, code, images, and docs](/images/de/resona-ai/ds-ai/attach-files.png)

### Supported File Types:

- **Spreadsheets (`.csv`, `.xlsx`, `.xls`)**: Upload partner sales exports, offline expense spreadsheets, or legacy BI dumps.
- **Documents (`.pdf`, `.docx`, `.txt`, `.md`)**: Attach quarterly business reviews, KPI requirement briefs, or investor decks.
- **Code & Database Scripts (`.sql`, `.ts`, `.py`, `.json`)**: Provide existing SQL scripts, Prisma/Drizzle schemas, or configuration objects.
- **Images (`.png`, `.jpg`, `.webp`)**: Attach wireframes, design mockups, or chart screenshots to guide the visual styling.

### Practical Use Cases:

- **Reconcile Discrepancies**: Upload a Stripe or Shopify CSV report and ask Dashboard AI to match it against your internal `orders` and `subscriptions` database tables.
- **Implement Business Briefs**: Upload a product manager's requirements PDF and ask: *"Build the executive dashboard specified on page 3 of this document."*
- **Replicate Dashboards**: Upload a screenshot of a legacy dashboard from another tool and ask Dashboard AI to rebuild it using your current PostgreSQL schema.

## 4 — Analytical Modes: Deep vs. Flash

Dashboard AI provides two distinct execution modes, with **Deep mode active by default**:

| Mode | Characteristics | When to Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Deep (Default)** | Multi-turn reasoning, multi-table joins, cross-schema correlation, statistical variance analysis, and written executive recommendations. | Comprehensive dashboard generation, anomaly root-cause investigation, and cohort analysis. |
| **Flash** | Ultra-fast single-turn execution, direct table queries, minimal credit usage. | Quick layout edits, renaming cards, changing chart colors, or simple scalar lookups. |

Switch between modes anytime using the dropdown selector in the bottom input bar.

## 5 — Initial Dashboard Generation

When you connect a database that has no dashboard defined yet, Dashboard AI presents a clear onboarding roadmap:

![Empty dashboard state explaining the 5 core AI capabilities before generating analytics](/images/de/resona-ai/ds-ai/empty-ds.png)

1. **Structure Understanding**: AI maps table relationships and data models automatically.
2. **Metric Identification**: AI identifies primary metrics, revenue streams, and behavioral cohorts.
3. **Read-Only Safety**: Strictly isolated queries ensure production data is never touched or modified.
4. **Executive Synthesis**: Translates raw rows into high-level KPIs and strategic narratives.
5. **Interactive Delivery**: Generates a persistent dashboard you and your team can interact with.

---

## 6 — Memory & Context Continuity

Dashboard AI is backed by Schema Weaver's [Memory System](/resona-ai/memory). It maintains context across conversation turns and remembers:

- Previously generated cards and layout customizations.
- Custom business metrics and domain formulas defined during earlier sessions.
- Pinned terminology and specific table filters.

If you refresh your browser or return tomorrow, Dashboard AI picks up right where you left off.
