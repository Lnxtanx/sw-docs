# Resona Voice

**Resona Voice** is Schema Weaver's conversational voice data analyst. Speak to your database naturally out loud — Resona listens, introspects your schema, queries your PostgreSQL tables through the local read-only connector, and speaks answers back to you in real time without requiring manual SQL writing or keyboard typing.

![Resona Voice connected and actively conversing in Data Explorer showing live speech bubbles, glowing voice orb, credit tracking, and session controls](/images/de/resona-ai/va/resona-voice-connected.png)

## 1 — Starting a Voice Session

You can start a Resona Voice session from anywhere in the application:

### Direct Launch from Resona AI Input Bar

Inside the [Resona AI Chat](/resona-ai/ai-chat) panel, look at the right side of the prompt input bar:

![Launching Resona Voice directly from the audio waveform button in the Resona AI prompt input bar](/images/de/resona-ai/va/resona-voice.png)

- Click the **audio waveform button** (tooltip: **`Start Resona Voice`**) or the microphone button.
- Your session initializes immediately without navigating away from your active work.
- You can also toggle Voice anytime using the global keyboard shortcut `Ctrl` / `Cmd` + `K`.

---

## 2 — The Connection & Orb Lifecycle

When a voice session begins, Resona establishes a low-latency, bidirectional audio stream. The dynamic orb reflects the agent's real-time state:

![Resona Voice initializing connection stage with amber Connecting status indicator and glowing orb](/images/de/resona-ai/va/connecting-stage.png)

| Orb State | Color & Indicator | What's Happening |
|---|---|---|
| **Connecting** | Amber pulsing orb (`Connecting...`) | Establishing secure WebRTC / WebSocket audio channel |
| **Listening** | Aquatic green/teal pulsing waves | Capturing your spoken words via microphone |
| **Thinking** | Shimmering violet/blue orb | Introspecting schema, formulating SQL queries, and analyzing row results |
| **Speaking** | Active teal concentric ripple | Streaming synthesized spoken response and updating live speech bubbles |
| **Idle** | Calming steady glow | Waiting for you or colleague to speak |

## 3 — Ambient & Persistent: Minimized Everywhere

A voice session is never trapped inside a single tab or dialog. When minimized, Resona Voice transforms into a compact, floating ambient companion that travels with you across every view in Schema Weaver.

### Floating on the Data Explorer Grid

Inspect raw records while conversing with your analyst:

![Resona Voice minimized as a floating orb on the Data Explorer table grid while browsing live rows](/images/de/resona-ai/va/va-on-de.png)

- Navigate tables, scroll through 500 / 1k / 2k rows, apply instant in-memory filters, or open full JSON/text inspection popovers.
- Speak aloud: *"What is the distribution of message types in this table?"* or *"Which user has the most records here?"*
- Resona queries the database in the background and answers into your headphones while your eyes stay focused on the grid.

### Floating on the Executive Dashboard

Examine high-level metrics with voice-driven drill-downs:

![Resona Voice minimized as a floating widget on the live Dashboard canvas with quick mic, mute, drag, and expand controls](/images/de/resona-ai/va/on-ds.png)

- **Drag to Move**: Hover over the widget (`Click to expand · Drag to move`) and drag it anywhere on screen so it never obstructs your charts, KPI cards, or cohort graphs.
- **One-Click Mute**: Click the red mic icon to instantly mute your microphone while keeping the audio session alive.
- **Expand Anytime**: Click the expand icon (`⤢`) to bring back the full-screen conversation view and transcript history.
- Ask questions about live visuals: *"Why did the daily average score dip on September 16?"* or *"Which subject has the highest accuracy?"*

## 4 — Conversational Experience & Capabilities

Resona Voice is engineered to converse like an experienced colleague rather than a robotic text-to-speech engine:

- **Natural Spoken Answers**: Resona synthesizes findings into concise, clear takeaways. It will never read raw row dumps or verbose SQL syntax at you over audio.
- **Barge-In Interruptibility**: You can interrupt at any moment. The instant you speak, Resona immediately stops talking, listens to your correction, and adjusts course.
- **Temporal & Relative Awareness**: The current timestamp is always part of the context window. Phrases like *"last Tuesday"*, *"this past quarter"*, or *"year-to-date"* resolve to exact SQL date ranges automatically.
- **Web & Domain Context**: If a query involves external context (such as industry benchmarks or public exchange rates), Voice can fetch live reference data.
- **Session Memory**: Checks your connection's [Memory Dossier](/resona-ai/memory) for saved KPI definitions, table aliases, and business rules.

## 5 — Voice Controls & Keyboard Mode

The floating voice control bar provides complete hands-free and hybrid control:

| Control | Action |
|---|---|
| **`+ Type`** | Opens a quick inline keyboard input to type numbers, spelling corrections, or specific email addresses without hanging up the call. |
| **Microphone Button** | Toggles voice recording on or off. |
| **Mute / Unmute** | Quick-mute toggle available directly on the minimized floating orb. |
| **Expand (`⤢`) / Minimize** | Switches between full conversation mode with transcript bubbles and ambient floating orb. |
| **End Session (`X` / Power)** | Safely terminates the audio connection and concludes credit tracking. You can also simply say *"Goodbye"* or *"End call"*. |

---

## 6 — Credit Usage

Voice interactions draw from your standard unified credit pool:

- Credits are metered transparently per conversational turn (visible on each message bubble, e.g. `29 credits used`, `269 credits used`).
- Voice queries share the exact same quota with [Resona AI Chat](/resona-ai/ai-chat) and [Dashboard AI](/resona-ai/dashboard).
- If your account reaches its monthly credit threshold, the session gently pauses and alerts you to top up or switch to manual grid browsing.

## When to Use Voice vs. Chat

| Capability | Resona Voice | Resona AI Chat |
|---|---|---|
| **Best For** | Fast exploratory questions, hands-free drill-downs while looking at charts or grids | Formal executive reports, downloadable exports, complex schema refactoring |
| **Interaction** | Hands-free natural speech, barge-in interrupts | Keyboard prompt, `@` table mentions, `+` file uploads |
| **Output** | Natural spoken audio + live transcript bubbles | Rich markdown tables, charts, CSV / Excel / PDF exports |
| **Presence** | Ambient floating orb (minimized across Grid & Dashboard) | Dedicated workspace and side copilot panel |
