# Data Explorer

The Data Explorer is where you work with live data. Connect a PostgreSQL database and you can browse it, ask it questions, or talk to it out loud.

---

## Three ways to work

Three top-level modes — move between them at any time without losing your place.

| Mode | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Data Explorer** | Read raw data in a high-performance grid — filter, sort, inspect |
| **Resona AI** | An autonomous agent that deep-dives your data and writes the report |
| **Dashboard** | One click, and it builds the executive dashboard from your database |

---

## Explore your data

Every table in your database, in a grid built for large ones. Filter with instant in-memory search or full database queries, customize page fetch sizes (from 100 to 2k rows), hover over any cell to read the full unclipped message or JSON without expanding, and inspect column statistics with a single click.

→ [Exploring Your Data](/data-explorer/exploring-data)

## Ask Resona AI

![Resona AI in Data Explorer with natural-language prompt bar, table suggestion chips, and voice launch shortcut](/images/de/resona-ai/de/de.png)

Resona is the assistant throughout Schema Weaver. It comes in a different form depending on where you are:

| Agent | Where it lives | What it's for |
|---|---|---|
| **Resona** | Full panel in Resona AI mode | Your main analyst — ask anything, get answers and files back |
| **Dashboard AI** | Panel beside your dashboard | Builds the dashboard and explains what each metric means |
| **Voice** | A floating orb, over every mode | Ask out loud instead of typing |

Depth is your choice — **Flash** for quick questions, **Deep** for multi-table analysis.

The voice orb is the one that follows you: start a session in the grid and it's still running when you switch to a chat or a dashboard.

→ [Resona AI](/resona-ai)

## Read-only by design

The connector authenticates locally and rejects anything that would modify your database, so exploring can never mutate production — whatever the AI generates. Your credentials never leave your environment.

→ [Security Architecture](/connector/security-architecture)

## Before you start

You need a PostgreSQL connection. Schema Weaver reaches your database through a connector that runs inside your environment, so it never holds your credentials.
