# Sharing with Your Team

A Connector can be shared with your team so colleagues can query the same databases you have. **They never see your credentials** — they get access to the Connector, which already has them.

---

## What a teammate gets

Access to every database that Connector reaches, through the Data Explorer:

- Browse tables and inspect column statistics
- Run read-only queries
- Ask Resona AI questions against your data
- Build dashboards

They cannot read or write your database credentials, because those never leave your Connector in the first place.

---

## What stays with the owner

Whoever registered the Connector keeps administrative control of it. In the **Settings** tab of the Connector Dashboard, owners can manage credentials and lifecycle options:

![Connector Settings view displaying connector host ownership, active registration status, and administrative revoke/remove controls](/images/de/connector/proxy-setting.png)

Administrative actions available to the owner:

- **Access Level**: Displays `Owner (You)` for the user who registered it. For teammates, management controls are disabled with a note explaining who owns the connector.
- **Registration & Activity**: Shows the registration date and the last active timestamp when queries or heartbeats were received.
- **Revoke Credentials**: Immediately invalidates the connection secret (`swagt_...`) to sever tunnel access without deleting connector configurations.
- **Remove Connector**: Unregisters the connector daemon and detaches all its databases from Schema Weaver.

If an owner leaves the team or you need to change hands, ownership has to be transferred from their account.

---

## Revoking a shared Connector

Revoking or removing a connector detaches it for everyone on the team — that's the behaviour to plan around if others depend on it for exploratory queries or shared dashboards.

Your local Connector installation on your host machine and its stored database passwords remain completely untouched either way.

---

## Managing shared Connectors in Team Settings

You can also view, toggle, or unshare connectors directly from the centralized team hub in **Settings → Collaboration → Connectors**:

![Collaboration Connectors tab displaying active local agent connector with shared status and unshare button](/images/de/team/sharing-connecots.png)

This gives team owners and admins a single view of all connector daemons contributing databases to the organization, including their live online heartbeats and agent IDs.

---

## Why not share credentials instead

Because there's no need to. A shared Connector gives the same access without anyone handling your database password or connection string. If a teammate leaves, you revoke their access without rotating a credential.
