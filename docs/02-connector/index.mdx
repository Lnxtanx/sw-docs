# The Connector

Schema Weaver reaches your database through **the Connector** — a small service you run yourself, on the machine that already has access to PostgreSQL.

Everything else in Schema Weaver depends on it. Browse a table, run a query, build a dashboard — all of it goes through the Connector first.

## The Connector Dashboard

The built-in Connector Dashboard gives you full visibility into your local daemons and database communications:

- **Connectors**: Active host connectors (e.g. `vivek-laptop-msi`) and their online state.
- **Relay Status**: Live outbound WebSocket tunnel heartbeat directly connecting your machine to Schema Weaver.
- **Response Latency**: Real-time roundtrip query latency tracking execution performance.
- **Query Activity**: Total executed statements and current execution success rate.
- **Recent Query Activity**: Live snapshot of incoming queries and schema probes with statement duration and timestamps.

---

## Why it runs on your side

Your database credentials are the sensitive part, so they never leave your environment. The Connector authenticates locally, and Schema Weaver talks to the Connector instead of to your database.

That's the whole idea: cloud orchestration on one side, your credentials on the other, and nothing in between holding both.

You can read how that boundary is enforced in [Security Architecture](/connector/security-architecture).

---

## What you put in

Exactly two values, both generated by the Connector itself:

| Value | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Connector ID** | Identifies this Connector to Schema Weaver. Starts with `agt_` |
| **Connection Key** | The secret that authorises it. Starts with `swagt_` |

That's it. **Schema Weaver never asks you for a database host, port, username, or password** — there is no field for it anywhere in the product. Your database details stay in the Connector's own configuration.

---

## What it does

- Holds your database credentials locally
- Keeps the connection open and reports whether it's healthy
- Runs the queries Schema Weaver asks for, and returns results
- Carries operational telemetry back, so you can see what ran

---

## What you'll need

- **Node.js 18 or newer**
- A PostgreSQL database reachable from the machine you run it on
- About five minutes
