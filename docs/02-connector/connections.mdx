# Your Connections

Each database your Connector can reach appears as a connection. You switch between them from the selector at the top of the Data Explorer.

---

## Picking a database

The selector groups your connections and marks which ones are online. Schema Weaver selects an available one for you when you arrive, and remembers your last choice for next time.

Only online connections are selectable — if the Connector is stopped, its databases won't appear as available.

![Discovered Databases dropdown in Schema Weaver Data Explorer showing online PostgreSQL instances routed through the host connector](/images/de/connector/connected-db.png)

The dropdown menu gives you:
- **Host provenance**: Shows which host connector serves each database (e.g. `via vivek-laptop-msi`).
- **Live status indicators**: Green status badges indicate healthy, online connections.
- **Direct shortcuts**: Quickly launch the **Connector Dashboard** or click **+ Connect database** to attach new instances.

---

## Discovered databases view

You can also inspect, search, and manage all databases across your active connectors from the **Databases** tab in the Connector Dashboard:

![Connector Databases management view listing all discovered PostgreSQL databases across host connectors with status indicators and quick Explore links](/images/de/connector/db-view.png)

From here, you can:
- **Search & Filter**: Filter discovered databases by name, alias, or host machine.
- **Inspect Connectivity**: Check real-time online status and host connector ID for each database.
- **One-Click Explore**: Click **Explore →** to immediately open any database in the Data Explorer without manually searching through menus.

---

## Adding more databases

The Connector can hold more than one. To add another:

```bash
pg-connector db add
```

Run it on the same machine as the existing Connector, then refresh. The new database appears in the selector alongside the others.

To reach something different, run a second Connector on a different machine and register it separately — each one is independent.

---

## Removing a connection

Revoking a Connector removes its registration and its databases from Schema Weaver. Your local Connector and its stored credentials are untouched — nothing on your machine changes.

If the Connector is shared with your team, see [Sharing with Your Team](/connector/sharing) first, since revoking affects everyone it's shared with.

---

## PostgreSQL only

Schema Weaver currently supports **PostgreSQL**. Other engines aren't supported yet, and there's nothing to configure — the connection type is fixed.

---

## What Schema Weaver stores

Just enough to show you the connection and route your queries: a display name, and whether it's currently online. Your host, port, username, password, and certificate never reach Schema Weaver at all.

[Security Architecture](/connector/security-architecture) covers the boundary in full.
