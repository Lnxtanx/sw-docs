# Plans and Credits

Three plans: **Free**, **Pro**, and **Team**. Resona works the same way on all of them — the difference is how many credits you get and what you can share with a team.

> Quick links: [Pricing Page](https://schemaweaver.dev/pricing) · [Contact Sales](mailto:support@schemaweaver.dev)

---

## Plans

| Plan | Price | Credits / month | Daily cap |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Free** | **$0** | 3,000 | None |
| **Pro** | **$29 / mo** | 200,000 | 20,000 |
| **Team** | **$30 / mo** (2 seats) | 100,000 per seat | 10,000 per seat |

---

## Team is priced per seat

The Team plan bills **$15 per seat**, with a **two-seat base** — so $30 covers the first two people, and each additional seat is $15.

Seats are how many people can use the workspace. Your subscription shows how many are in use and how many remain, and owners can add or remove seats from the team billing settings.

---

## What each plan includes

| | Free | Pro | Team |
|---|---|---|---|
| **AI credits** | 3,000 / mo | 200,000 / mo | 100,000 per seat |
| **Daily cap** | None | 20,000 | 10,000 per seat |
| **Flash & Deep modes** | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| **Voice AI (speech queries)** | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| **Team members** | Join by invite | Up to 5 | By seat |
| **Shared database connections** | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| **Support** | Community | Priority | Priority |

**Pro** includes team sharing for up to 5 people, but each member's credits are their own — there's no shared pool.

**Team** is what makes the workspace genuinely collaborative: connections and workspaces are shared across everyone on the team. Credits are still **per seat** here too, so each person gets their own 100,000 rather than drawing from one shared balance.

---

## What credits are

Credits are what Resona costs to run. Every message, query, and generated report draws from the balance, and all of your surfaces — chat, voice, and dashboards — draw from the same one.

**Free** has no daily cap: the whole 3,000 is available whenever you want it. Every plan resets at the start of the billing month, and unused credits don't carry over.

## Monitoring credit usage (`Settings → AI Usage`)

To check your current credit consumption, remaining balances, and daily resets, navigate to the **AI Usage** panel in the Settings menu:

1. Click the gear icon or your profile avatar in the bottom-left sidebar to open **Settings**.
2. Select **AI Usage** from the left-hand navigation.

![Schema Weaver Settings showing AI Credit Usage with monthly and daily progress bars, concurrent agents capacity, and plan tier](/images/de/resona-ai/ussage-ai-credit.png)

### Key metrics tracked:

- **Monthly credits** — Displays total credits consumed in the current billing cycle against your monthly allowance (e.g., `364 / 100K credits`, `0% used`, `99.6K credits remaining`), along with a breakdown of credits used today.
- **Daily safety cap** — Displays today's usage against your plan's daily limit (e.g., `364 / 10K credits`, `4% used`, `9.6K credits remaining`) alongside a live countdown timer until daily quota reset (e.g., `Resets in 12h 50m`).
- **Concurrent agents** — Reflects active parallel execution capacity (e.g., `0 / 2`), indicating how many simultaneous agentic workflows can run in parallel.
- **Plan tier** — Identifies your active subscription tier (`Team`, `Pro`, or `Free`).
- **Live refresh** — Click the refresh icon in the top header anytime to get up-to-the-minute credit metrics.
