# Team Collaboration

Collaboration in Schema Weaver is built around **teams**. Create a team, invite your colleagues, share your local agent connectors, and collaborate on shared AI projects and databases — with zero credential exposure.

![Settings Collaboration modal displaying Active Team selector, Owner permissions, seat quota badge, and Member invitation panel](/images/de/team/collabrtion.png)

> Quick links: [Plans & Credits](/pricing) · [Connector Sharing](/connector/sharing) · [Security Architecture](/connector/security-architecture)

---

## 1 — Collaboration Hub (`Settings → Collaboration`)

Open **Settings → Collaboration** to access your team control center:

- **Active Team Switcher**: Switch between organizations or create a new team anytime using **`+ New Team`**.
- **Your Permission**: Clear indicator of your team authority (`Owner`, `Admin`, or `Member`).
- **Seat Quotas & Limits**: Displays current seat usage (e.g., `Seats 2/2`). If your seat capacity is reached, an alert prompts the team owner to upgrade on [Plans & Credits](/pricing) before sending additional invites.
- **Four Core Sub-Tabs**:
  1. **`Members`**: Invite colleagues and manage member roles.
  2. **`Connectors`**: Share or unshare local connectors.
  3. **`Share Projects`**: Distribute AI workspaces and saved chats.
  4. **`Shared DBs`**: View all team-accessible databases.

---

## 2 — Members & Role Management

Under the **`Members`** tab, owners and admins can invite new teammates and govern permissions:

### Inviting Teammates

1. Enter your colleague's corporate email address (`colleague@company.com`).
2. Select their role (**Member** by default, or **Admin**).
3. Click **Invite**. 

If the invited person does not yet have a Schema Weaver account, the email invite guides them through account setup. No pre-existing account is required.

### Roles and Permissions

| Role | What They Can Do |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | Full authority — manage billing, upgrade seat counts, invite and remove members, change roles, share/disconnect projects and connectors, and delete the team. |
| **Admin** | Manage team members, pending invitations, projects, and shared connectors. Cannot alter subscription billing or delete the team. |
| **Member** | Browse shared databases in the Data Explorer, run read-only queries, converse with Resona AI, and participate in shared AI projects. |

## 3 — Sharing Connectors

Under the **`Connectors`** tab, you can grant team members access to databases reaching through your local Connector daemon:

![Collaboration Connectors tab displaying active local connector with shared status and unshare button](/images/de/team/sharing-connecots.png)

- **One-Click Share/Unshare**: Toggle whether your registered connector daemon (e.g. `vivek-laptop-msi`) is shared with the active team.
- **Agent Identity & Health**: Displays the unique agent ID (`agt_vivek-laptop-msi_12662b69`) and a live heartbeat indicator (green status dot) showing whether the daemon is online.
- **Zero Credential Sharing**: Teammates receive tunnel access to execute read-only queries through your connector, but your database passwords, hosts, and secrets **never leave your machine**.

→ Learn more in [Sharing with Your Team](/connector/sharing).

## 4 — Sharing AI Projects

Under the **`Share Projects`** tab, team members can collaborate on conversational analyses, dashboards, and research workspaces:

![Collaboration Share Projects tab showing AI Projects like cloop-dataset with Share with Team action](/images/de/team/ai-rproject-sharing.png)

- **Project Workspaces**: Select any private project (e.g., `cloop-dataset` General Workspace) and click **Share with Team**.
- **Collaborative History**: Once shared, teammates can review conversation history, inspect generated artifacts, re-run queries, and build on existing analyses without duplicating effort.
- **Unified Memory**: Directives and metric definitions saved in the project's memory dossier become immediately accessible to all team members using [Resona AI](/resona-ai), [Resona Voice](/resona-ai/voice), and [Dashboard AI](/resona-ai/dashboard).

---

## 5 — Browsing Shared Databases

Under the **`Shared DBs`** tab, team members can see every database currently accessible to the team:

![Collaboration Shared DBs tab displaying all 5 shared databases accessible via local connectors with live status indicators](/images/de/team/db-sharing.png)

- **Centralized Inventory**: Shows all databases currently tunneled through shared connectors (e.g., `migration-engine-testing`, `schema-weaver`, `sw-analytics`, `postgres`, `cloopdb`).
- **Live Status & Agent Provenance**: Each card shows an active online status indicator, the host daemon machine name, and an `AGENT` badge.
- **Instant Availability**: Any database listed here immediately appears in the Data Explorer connection dropdown for all team members.

## 6 — Seats and Billing Governance

On paid plans, seats govern team capacity:

| | Owner | Admin | Member |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Invite & Remove Members** | ✓ | ✓ | — |
| **Change Member Roles** | ✓ | ✓ | — |
| **Share & Unshare Connectors** | ✓ | ✓ | — |
| **Share & Unlink Projects** | ✓ | ✓ | — |
| **Manage Seats & Billing** | ✓ | — | — |
| **Delete Team** | ✓ | — | — |

- The **Team Plan** includes a **two-seat base** ($30/mo) with each additional seat at $15/mo.
- Each seat receives its own dedicated **100,000 monthly credits** pool and **10,000 daily cap**, ensuring one teammate's queries never exhaust another teammate's quota.
